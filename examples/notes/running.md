# Marathon training

## Easy runs
80% of weekly mileage should be at an easy, conversational pace, roughly zone 2 heart rate. Easy running builds mitochondrial density and capillaries without the recovery cost of hard sessions.

## Long run
The long run builds to 32-35 km about four weeks before race day. Practise race nutrition on long runs: 60-90 g of carbohydrate per hour from gels or drinks, so the gut is trained.

## Tapering
Cut volume by about 40-60% over the final two to three weeks while keeping some intensity. Fitness does not drop in that window; fatigue does. Expect to feel sluggish in the first taper week.

## Carb loading
Two to three days before the race, eat 8-10 g of carbohydrate per kg of body weight per day to fill glycogen stores. Reduce fibre to avoid gut trouble on race morning.

## Injury prevention
Most running injuries come from increasing mileage too fast. Keep weekly increases around 10% and add strength work for calves, hips and glutes twice a week. Replace shoes every 600-800 km.
