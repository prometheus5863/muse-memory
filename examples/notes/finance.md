# Personal finance

## Emergency fund
Keep three to six months of expenses in a high-yield savings account before investing. It is insurance, not an investment, so its return does not matter much.

## Index funds
Low-cost broad market index funds beat most actively managed funds after fees over long periods. Expense ratio matters: a 1% fee compounds into a large fraction of final wealth over 30 years.

## Asset allocation
Allocation between stocks and bonds drives most of portfolio volatility. Rebalance once a year or when an asset class drifts more than 5 percentage points from target. Rebalancing forces selling high and buying low.

## Compounding
Money grows exponentially with time, so starting early matters more than contribution size. Rule of 72: divide 72 by the annual return to estimate years to double; at 7% that is about ten years.
