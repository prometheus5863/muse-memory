# Home ferments

## Kimchi
Napa cabbage salted at about 2% by weight for 2 hours, rinsed, then mixed with gochugaru, garlic, ginger, fish sauce and scallions. Leave at room temperature 1-2 days, then refrigerate. Lactobacillus does the work; the brine drops below pH 4.6 within a couple of days which keeps pathogens out.

## Kombucha
Kombucha uses a SCOBY, a symbiotic culture of bacteria and yeast. First ferment is sweet black tea for 7-10 days. Second ferment in sealed bottles with fruit for 2-3 days builds carbonation. Burp bottles daily to avoid explosions.

## Yoghurt
Heat milk to 85°C to denature proteins (thicker yoghurt), cool to 43°C, add two spoonfuls of the previous batch, hold warm for 8 hours. Longer incubation makes it more sour as lactic acid bacteria keep converting lactose.

## Fermentation safety
Salt concentration, acidity and keeping vegetables submerged under brine are the three controls. Kahm yeast on top is harmless but tastes off; fuzzy coloured mould means throw it out.
